Our Story

About iFly Airlines

I Fly Air Solutions Ltd was established in 2018 by a team led by Mr. Abdullahi Adan Hassan (CEO). The company started as a travel agency named I Fly Travel, whose network with all major airlines grew because of its diverse connections in air ticket sales.

The company opened sales offices across the country — Wajir, Eldoret, Lodwar, Malindi, Mombasa, Lamu and Eastleigh — to bring services closer to clients. Seeing the deficit in the market, the management changed its name to I Fly Air Solutions Ltd in December 2019 and was issued an Air Service License in 2020.

Travel Information

Before You Fly

Everything you need to know to prepare for your iFly journey.

Baggage Policy

Understand what you can carry on board and in the hold, and how to prepare your luggage for check-in.

View Details
  • Hand luggage: 1 piece up to 5 kg
  • Checked baggage: up to 15 kg included
  • Excess baggage charges apply per kg
  • Fragile items must be declared at check-in
  • Oversized items subject to approval

Dangerous Goods

Certain items are prohibited or restricted on board. Familiarise yourself with the rules to ensure a smooth journey.

View Details
  • Explosives, fireworks & flares strictly prohibited
  • Flammable liquids & gases not allowed
  • Lithium batteries: hand-luggage only (≤100Wh)
  • Corrosives, oxidizers & toxic substances banned
  • Magnetic materials must be packed safely

FAQs

Answers to the most common questions about booking, check-in, baggage and flying with iFly.

Common Questions
  • Check-in opens 2 hours before departure
  • Valid ID required for all passengers
  • Infants under 2 may travel on lap (with ticket)
  • Reschedule or cancellation — contact reservations
  • Pets not accepted in cabin

Ticket Policies

Understand fare conditions, change fees, refund policies and ticket validity before you book.

View Details
  • Tickets are non-transferable
  • Name changes attract a service fee
  • Same-day rescheduling subject to availability
  • No-shows forfeit the full ticket value
  • Group booking discounts available on request

Travel Requirements

Entry requirements vary by destination. Check visa, health and documentation rules before you travel.

View Details
  • Valid passport or national ID required
  • Juba: South Sudan visa required for most nationalities
  • Mogadishu: Somalia visa on arrival available
  • Yellow fever certificate may be required
  • Check destination health advisories before flying

Travelling with Children

We welcome families on board. Here is what to know when travelling with infants or young children.

View Details
  • Infants (under 2): lap infant ticket required
  • Children (2–11): child fare applies
  • Unaccompanied minors must be pre-arranged
  • Strollers accepted as checked baggage
  • Priority boarding for families with infants
